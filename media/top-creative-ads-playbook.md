# Top Creative Ads Playbook (TikTok + YouTube)

This doc lists high-impact creative ads and a practical prompt to create a similar-style ad concept.

## TikTok Ads

| Name | Link | Prompt to create a similar ad |
|---|---|---|
| e.l.f. Cosmetics — #eyeslipsface | https://www.tiktok.com/tag/eyeslipsface | Create a 15-second TikTok ad for **[product]** with an original, catchy audio hook and a simple participation challenge. Use fast cuts, bold text overlays, and a clear hashtag challenge CTA: “Show us your version with #[brandchallenge].” Keep it trend-native, playful, and UGC-first. |
| Duolingo — Mascot-led trend content | https://www.tiktok.com/@duolingo | Create a TikTok skit where a bold brand mascot reacts to a trending sound in an unexpected way. Script 3 beats: hook in first 2 seconds, humor twist in middle, and a soft CTA at end (“Try [product] in bio”). Keep it low-polish, meme-native, and comment-friendly. |
| Chipotle — #LidFlip challenge | https://www.tiktok.com/tag/lidflip | Create a challenge-style TikTok ad around one satisfying physical action tied to **[product]**. Show the move clearly, invite duets/remixes, and add on-screen challenge rules in one sentence. End with “Can you beat this?” plus hashtag and creator-friendly callout. |
| Netflix — #WhatsYourPower | https://www.tiktok.com/tag/whatsyourpower | Create a cinematic TikTok campaign concept where users reveal their “power” using a branded effect. Deliver a dramatic hook, short transformation moment, and CTA to “Show your power with #[hashtag].” Blend storytelling with easy replication for creators. |
| NYC Votes — creator-led civic campaign | https://www.tiktok.com/@nycvotes | Create a creator-collab TikTok ad for **[cause/product]** using 3 local creator POVs in one montage. Tone should feel authentic, local, and action-oriented. Include one myth-busting line, one emotional line, and one clear action CTA. |
| RSPB — Bird of the Week format | https://www.tiktok.com/@rspb | Create a recurring TikTok mini-series ad (“[Topic] of the Week”) with game-show energy. Use a host voice, quick fact reveal, and playful ranking format. End with “Comment your pick for next week” to drive retention and serial engagement. |
