# PikAppoint Demo Video — Narration Script

**Total Duration:** ~90-120 seconds
**Voice:** Bark TTS `v2/en_speaker_6` (neutral professional, fully offline)
**Max segment length:** 20s spoken text — split longer narrations into multiple files (Bark cutoff rule)

---

## Step 1 — Provider: Create an Opening (scene1a + scene1b)

**Visual:** Provider logs into dashboard, navigates to Opening page, clicks "Add Opening" button, fills form (date, start time, end time, service, worker), clicks Save. Opening appears on calendar grid.

### scene1a
> "Welcome to PikAppoint. Let's see how providers create openings. From the admin dashboard, navigate to the Opening page. Click 'Add Opening' to schedule a new time slot."

### scene1b
> "Fill in the date, start time, end time, service type, and worker name. Click Save. Your opening now appears on the calendar, ready for customers to book."

---

## Step 2 — Customer: Book the Opening (scene2a + scene2b)

**Visual:** Customer views available openings browse page, filters by service/date, selects an opening, clicks Book. Confirmation dialog appears. Customer clicks Confirm. Booking pending approval.

### scene2a
> "Customers can browse available openings on the booking page. They can filter by service, date, or location."

### scene2b
> "When they find a slot, they click Book. A confirmation dialog shows the appointment details. Click Confirm, and the reservation is now pending provider approval."

---

## Step 3 — Provider: Confirm the Reservation (scene3)

**Visual:** Provider navigates to Appointments tab, sees pending reservation with yellow badge, clicks on it to view details, clicks "Approve" button. Status changes to "Confirmed" with green badge.

**Narration:**
> "Back on the provider side, navigate to the Appointments tab. Pending reservations show with a yellow badge. Click on the appointment to review the details, then click Approve. The status changes to Confirmed."

---

## Step 4 — Provider: Complete the Reservation (scene4)

**Visual:** After service is delivered, provider returns to Appointments tab, finds the confirmed reservation, clicks "Mark Complete". Status changes to "Completed". Payment proof upload option appears (optional).

**Narration:**
> "After the service is delivered, find the confirmed reservation in your Appointments tab and click Mark Complete. The status updates to Completed. You can optionally upload payment proof for record keeping. That's the full booking flow in PikAppoint."
